Standardized pediatric visual acuity test panels, color-contrast recognition, and calibrated pure-tone audiometric evaluations to spot early school performance impairments.

12-Lead Electrocardiogram

Diagnostic Digital EKG (Thoracic Evaluation)

Immediate high-resolution digital resting 12-lead electrocardiography. Physician-interpreted analysis for palpitations, suspected arrhythmias, hypertension follow-ups, and athletic clearance.

Biochemical Panel

Anemia & Diabetes Metabolic Screening

Targeted blood workup targeting common regional nutritional deficiencies: complete blood count (CBC), serum ferritin, iron saturation indices, HbA1c, and fasting glucose metrics.

Notice on Clinical Workflow & Consultation Protocols

"Additional procedures strictly follow doctor's consultation." To avoid over-investigation, unnecessary patient expenses, and inappropriate pharmaceutical exposure, all diagnostic tests, injections, and therapy referrals are administered exclusively following an initial direct physical physician evaluation.

Track A • Corporate Health

Corporate & Institutional Health

Structured occupational medicine conforming to both Kyrgyz labor sanitary mandates and rigorous international standards. Designed to minimize workforce downtime and ensure preventive wellness.

  • Annual Staff Health Check-ups: Preventive cardiovascular, metabolic, ophthalmic, and pulmonary assessments conducted on-site or at our Tunguch facility.
  • Embassy & Expat Staff Packages: Western-aligned protocol consultations, trilingual medical documentation (EN/RU/KY), and direct coordination for international insurance claims.
  • International School Health Screenings: Pediatric physicals, developmental screenings, sports safety clearances, immunization status checks, and school nurse supervisory support.
Track B • Medical Education

Medical Education & Training

Cultivating the next generation of healthcare leaders in Central Asia through evidence-based mentorship, hands-on clinical electives, and structured international curriculum workshops.

  • Training for Doctors & Medical Students: Direct shadowing alongside senior bilingual consultants, outpatient diagnostic rounds, and point-of-care ultrasound training.
  • Continuous Clinical Seminars & Grand Rounds: Bi-weekly multidisciplinary case presentations reviewing complex cardiovascular, pediatric, and endocrinological patient trajectories.
  • Residency Clinical Electives: 4-to-12 week structured rotations for international and Kyrgyz medical university residents in outpatient internal medicine and pediatrics.
