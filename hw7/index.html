Our Story

About MercyMed Bishkek

A private medical centre built on evidence-based medicine, international experience, and compassionate whole-family care.

Our History

The MercyMed project began over 10 years ago with charitable mobile clinics across remote Kyrgyzstan oblasts. Our Bishkek clinic opened in October 2023 to provide a stable base for follow-up care and a permanent team.

Mobile clinics remain the heart of MercyMed — every year our team travels to villages that invite us, providing consultations, medications, and humanitarian relief.

How We Work

We follow evidence-based guidelines from WHO and the Ministry of Health of the Kyrgyz Republic. Every treatment plan is reviewed by our bilingual clinical faculty and explained in detail to each patient.

Our promise: fewer unnecessary tests, rational prescribing, and transparent communication in your language.

Track A • Corporate Health

Corporate & Institutional Health

Structured occupational medicine conforming to both Kyrgyz labor sanitary mandates and rigorous international standards.

  • Annual Staff Health Check-ups: Preventive cardiovascular, metabolic, ophthalmic, and pulmonary assessments on-site or at our Tunguch facility.
  • Embassy & Expat Staff Packages: Western-aligned protocol consultations, trilingual medical documentation, and insurance claim coordination.
  • School Health Screenings: Pediatric physicals, developmental screenings, sports clearances, and immunization status checks.
Track B • Medical Education

Medical Education & Training

Cultivating the next generation of healthcare leaders in Central Asia through evidence-based mentorship and clinical electives.

  • Training for Doctors & Students: Shadowing alongside senior bilingual consultants, outpatient diagnostic rounds, and point-of-care ultrasound training.
  • Clinical Seminars & Grand Rounds: Bi-weekly multidisciplinary case presentations reviewing complex patient trajectories.
  • Residency Clinical Electives: 4-to-12 week structured rotations for international and Kyrgyz medical residents in outpatient internal medicine and pediatrics.
